# ACE Framework

> A universal thinking space organization system by Nick Milo (Linking Your Thinking).

## The Three Pillars

### 🗺️ Atlas — Things to Know
Your knowledge base. Reference materials, concepts, evergreen notes, and maps of content live here. Atlas is where processed, refined thinking goes to be preserved and connected.

### ⚡ Efforts — Things to Do  
Your active work. Projects (Active, Simmering, Sleeping) and Areas of Effort organize what you're working on, what you might work on, and what's parked for later.

### 📅 Calendar — Make It Whole
Your timeline. Daily notes, meetings, and time-based reflections create continuity across time and help you see patterns in your thinking and productivity.

## Supporting Sections

### ➕ Plus — Capture What's New
Your inbox. Every new idea and quick capture lands here first. Process regularly and move items to their permanent home in Atlas, Efforts, or Calendar.

### 📦 Extras — The Overflow
Miscellaneous items, attachments, and things that don't fit neatly in the three pillars.

## Core Principle

> *"Everything has a place in ACE, a universal thinking space."*

The ACE framework provides cognitive relief because you always know:
1. **Where to find** information
2. **Where to put** new information
